# International Workshop on Constraint Modelling and Reformulation

This is the archive of the **ModRef** workshop series. It collects the history of
the series and hosts a [searchable list of all past contributions](files/) with
their papers and slides.

Recent editions each have their own site (`YEAR.modref.org`), linked below. This
page supersedes the series page previously maintained at
`www-users.york.ac.uk/~af9/ModRef`, which is no longer updated; some older external
links below point to third-party pages that may no longer be available.

## Organisation

Each event has been organised by a Programme Chair and Programme Committee, who are
listed with each event. In addition, the series has been overseen by a Steering
Chair. Those serving this role are:

- 2002–2017: Alan Frisch
- 2018–2019: Kevin Leo and Alan Frisch
- 2020–present: Mateu Villaret
